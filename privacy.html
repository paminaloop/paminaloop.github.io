2. Overview

Arrowly is an arrow puzzle game. No account and no sign-in are required. Your progress, coins, boosters, cosmetic unlocks and settings are stored only locally on your device; the provider does not receive this data.

The app is funded by ads from Google AdMob. Google processes personal data for this (e.g. IP address, advertising ID, device information). Where required by law (in particular in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app first asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform (“UMP”).

Arrowly does not use its own analytics or tracking system.

3. Data stored locally on your device

The app stores in its private storage on your device:

  • game progress (solved levels, daily puzzles, interrupted puzzles, statistics such as solved puzzles and play time),
  • coin balance, coins earned and spent, booster charges, rewards granted for videos (e.g. coin bonuses already used), pending hints,
  • purchased and equipped cosmetic items (bought with game coins, no real money),
  • settings (language, theme, sound, music, vibration) and explanations already shown,
  • the age group you selected (under 16, 16 or 17, 18 and over – no date of birth; see section 7).

This data does not leave the device and is not collected by the provider. Game coins are not a currency and can neither be bought nor paid out.

Retention: until you clear the app data or uninstall the app. Android may include the data in the Android backup you have enabled (Google account); you control this in the Android settings.

5. Consent management (Google User Messaging Platform)

When the app starts, it uses the UMP SDK (version 4.0.0 for Android) to check whether consent is required for your region and, if so, shows Google's consent form. Only afterwards – and only if the requirements are met – does the app request ads.

  • For this, the IP address (to determine the region) and device information are sent to Google.
  • If you selected “under 16”, the app does not ask for consent (see section 7).
  • Your choice (e.g. as a TCF consent string) is stored locally on the device and passed on to Google and the ad partners so that they respect your decision.
  • Change or withdraw consent: at any time under Settings → Privacy options (shown when privacy options apply to your region). Withdrawal takes effect for the future.

Legal basis: compliance with the legal obligation to obtain and document consent (Art. 6(1)(c) in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR); storing your choice on the device is strictly necessary for this (Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).

Retention: until you change your choice, clear the app data or uninstall the app; Google may ask again after some time.

6. No other data transfers

  • No account, no sign-in, no real-money in-app purchases (as of version 4.3.0).
  • No own analytics, no own tracking, no transfer of game data to the provider.
  • The app uses the Unity game engine. Unity Analytics, Unity Cloud Diagnostics (crash reports), Unity Ads and Unity hardware statistics are disabled; the app is not connected to any Unity Cloud project.
  • Google Play (installation, updates, crash reports via the Play Console) is subject to Google's terms and privacy policy.

7. Children and teenagers

Arrowly is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not specifically directed at children under 13.

When you first start the app, it asks for your age group. Only the selected group is stored, locally on your device; it is not sent to the provider. Until you have chosen, the app does not request any ads. You can change your answer at any time under Settings → Privacy & licenses → Change age. The group determines the ad settings:

  • Under 16: no consent request. The app marks its ad requests to Google as requests for users under the age of consent with child-appropriate treatment. No personalized ads are served, and ads are limited to the content rating “G” (general audiences).
  • 16 and 17: consent form as for adults, but no personalized ads (teen treatment); content rating at most “PG”.
  • 18 and over: personalized ads only with your consent in the consent form; content rating at most “PG”.
